# Allocation App

## Problem Statement

Teams need a simple way to assign tasks to the people who will do them, and for
those people to see what they have been given without chasing a manager for
updates.

## Solution

A bare-minimum task allocation app: an admin assigns tasks to team members,
and each team member signs in to see their own assigned tasks.

## Actors

- **Admin** — assigns tasks to team members and sees all allocations.
- **Team Member** — signs in and sees the tasks assigned to them.
